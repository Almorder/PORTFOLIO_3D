# Design System V14 — Fabrica structure × Nolan Arc identity

## What stays from Fabrica
- Strong information hierarchy.
- Large media and project-first composition.
- Controlled whitespace: space must frame a proof, a decision or an action.
- Minimal navigation and clear page roles.
- Grain as a continuous material, not a decorative block.

## Nolan Arc identity restored
### Palette
- Warm black: `#080706`
- Secondary black/brown: `#100D0B`
- Ivory: `#F0EBE2`
- Warm paper: `#E6DDD1`
- Orange: `#CC460C`
- Soft orange: `#E97736`

### Type
- **Yrsa**: wordmark, major headings, editorial voice, emotional emphasis.
- **Syne**: navigation, labels, UI, structural titles and categories.
- **DM Sans**: paragraphs, forms and information that must be read quickly.
- **Courier/system monospace**: optional technical micro-information only.

## Geometry
- Rounded media and containers, generally 20–38px.
- Glass surfaces only where interaction benefits from depth.
- No hard diagonal structural language.
- Thin orange accents rather than decorative lines everywhere.

## Signature components
- Fabrica-style preloader structure, recolored and retyped Nolan Arc.
- Glassy Button.
- Animated Stats Pro engine.
- Focus Testimonials in flowing text mode.
- Glass Showcase / journey.
- Ambient Video Player + Hold Confirm.
- Stacked Flow.
- Video Slide Show with inertia.
- Vertical Line Menu TOC on desktop.
- Gradient Motion backgrounds used locally.
- Mobile glass dock: Home / Work / Services / Contact.

## Mobile rule
Mobile is not desktop compressed. The header, dock, menus, title sizes, media ratios, form density and scroll length are explicitly adjusted below 720px.
